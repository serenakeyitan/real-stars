Data straight from the ICSE 2026 StarScout paper — peer-reviewed, snapshot from 2025-01-01. We did not compute these numbers ourselves; we just put them on a wall. Each repo was flagged by one or both of StarScout's heuristics:

  • low-activity: stargazer accounts that only ever starred this one repository (the throwaway-account fingerprint of cheap fake-star sellers)
  • lockstep: groups of 50+ accounts that hit many repos together in tight time windows (sophisticated farm behavior)

90% of the repos StarScout flagged were later deleted by GitHub Trust & Safety — the strongest external validation any fake-star detector has.
