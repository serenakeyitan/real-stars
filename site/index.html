How they were caught

heuristic 1

Low activity

Accounts that starred this one repository and nothing else. The throwaway-account fingerprint of cheap fake-star sellers.

heuristic 2

Lockstep

Groups of 50+ accounts that hit many repos together in tight time windows. Sophisticated farm behavior, much harder to fake by hand.

validation

90% later banned

GitHub's Trust & Safety team has since deleted 90% of the accounts StarScout flagged. The strongest external signal any detector has produced.
